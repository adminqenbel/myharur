What MyHarur does

MyHarur is a community app for the residents of Harur and Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu, built and maintained by QenBel. It brings together moderated local news, live weather, civic issue reports (road, electricity, water, government), community events, local job postings and emergency helpline numbers — every post is reviewed by a human moderator before it goes live.

Why we ask for your Google account

Signing in with Google lets us verify you are a real person before you can post to a shared community feed, which keeps the app free of spam and fake accounts. We only receive your name, e-mail address and profile photo from Google — we never post, message or take any action on Google's platforms on your behalf, and we never see your Google password.
